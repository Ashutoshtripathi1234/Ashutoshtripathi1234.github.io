I am a Senior Associate Data Scientist (L1) at Publicis Sapient, Bengaluru, India where I build state-of-the-art AI-driven compliance systems. My work spans agentic workflows, LLM-based validation, and multi-modal content compliance for the Bodhi Product.

I graduated with a B.Tech in Electronics Engineering from Rajiv Gandhi Institute of Petroleum Technology (RGIPT), Jais, UP (2021–2025). I have been fortunate to collaborate on research with Sriparna Saha (scholar) at IIT Patna and Liangqiong Qu (scholar) at The University of Hong Kong in Federated Learning and Medical Image Classification, resulting in publications at top venues including MICCAI 2024, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, IEEE Transactions on AI, and TMLR.

I am always open to research collaborations in Generative AI, Agentic Systems, and Federated Learning.
